good to know

Parks, Permits & Weather

Parking & passes
  • Silver Falls and Ecola both charge day-use parking, no advance purchase needed
  • Pay on arrival at the yellow fee machines or by scanning the QR code posted at the lot
  • $10 per day for Oregon residents, $12 for non-residents
  • Other state parks may charge where posted
  • Cannon Beach & Haystack Rock need no beach pass
  • No federal park pass required
  • Carry a card and phone for the fee machines and QR payments
If the weather turns
  • Shops and galleries downtown
  • Coffee stops
  • Fireplace, balcony, spa tub
  • Scenic drives, only if roads are safe
  • Longer, unhurried meals
Tuesday is left intentionally soft so it can absorb anything weather pushes from earlier days. Trail of Ten Falls stays on Saturday unless there's a real safety or closure issue.
